## 🚀 Projects

### 🍽️ Restaurant Operations Suite — Düşbərə, Lankaran

**Central Warehouse System**
- Tracks a shared central warehouse across 4 outlets: Kitchen, Bar (Spirits/Beer), Kebab House and Ailəvi House
- Cash reconciliation split across two registers (Düşbərə combined + Ailəvi House)
- Restaurants place stock orders via a **Telegram bot** → orders land automatically in a *Restaurant Orders* section
- Sales side runs on **1C:Enterprise**
- Built to be sold to other restaurants: **one site + one isolated database per client**

**Payroll Systems (×2)**
- Two payroll apps (Ailəvi House & Düşbərə) sharing one code structure
- Attendance, work schedules, bonuses and penalties
- Manual carry-over of previous debt per employee (replaces advance payments)
- Departing employees are **archived, never deleted** · date-based search · password login
- Role-based access: **Admin** sees everything; **Manager** records attendance and adds staff, but cannot see salary amounts

**Receipt Cancellation Audit**
- Daily AI-assisted workflow: screenshots of the 1C "Receipt Cancellations" screen are aggregated by section (Hall 1, Staff, Cabins Row 1/2/3, Cashier) into an Excel report

### 🫖 Çayxor — Tea House Management App *(latest)*

- Manages tables, orders, menu, inventory, purchasing and expenses
- Mobile-first, built on the free tiers of **Firebase + Netlify**
- Owner-only access with **PIN login**; the owner records staff attendance and daily pay
- **Daily report to Telegram via GitHub Actions** — sent when the night shift closes, or automatically at 02:00 if it hasn't
- Business-day logic: the shop runs 08:00 → 02:00/03:00, and post-midnight sales roll into the previous day

### 🤖 Automation with n8n *(in progress)*

- **News Bot** — every morning at 9:00 pulls the top 5 world stories from RSS feeds (e.g. BBC World), translates them into Azerbaijani with Claude, and posts to Telegram
- **YouTube Automation** — send a topic to Telegram → AI produces a full video (voice + visuals) → uploads it to YouTube

### ✈️ Heydar Aliyev International Airport — Analytics Case
